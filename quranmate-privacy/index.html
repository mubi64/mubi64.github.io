QuranMate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

QuranMate is a free Quran reading and memorization app published by Sowaan (Private) Limited (“we”, “us”). QuranMate keeps everything on your device. We do not collect your personal data. This page explains what the app does with the data on your phone, which outside services it talks to, and how the voice feature handles your voice.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Your bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading statistics and Hifz progress stay on your phone.
  • The app downloads the Quran text once, and streams recitation audio only when you play it.
  • Voice reveal in Hifz mode is optional. QuranMate never records, saves or sends your voice to us. How the recognition itself is done depends on the option you pick: see “Microphone and voice reveal”.

What stays on your phone

Your bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading statistics (pages, time, streak), Hifz progress and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere and are removed when you uninstall the app. We cannot see them and cannot recover them for you.

What the app downloads

On first launch it downloads the Quran text and translations once from a public GitHub repository (raw.githubusercontent.com/fawazahmed0/quran-api). Recitation audio is streamed from everyayah.com only when you press play, and can be saved on your device for offline listening. These servers see an ordinary web request (your IP address and the app's user-agent) as any website would; QuranMate sends them nothing else about you and we do not receive or store those requests.

Microphone and voice reveal

If you use voice reveal in Hifz mode, the app asks for microphone access (and, if you use your device's speech service, speech-recognition access). It uses the microphone only while voice reveal is on, to follow your recitation and reveal the lines you recite. QuranMate does not record, save or send your voice to us. There are two ways recognition can run. The first time you use voice reveal, the app offers a choice: download the offline model, or use your device's speech service for that session. Once the offline model has been downloaded, voice reveal uses it. If the model cannot be loaded, the app falls back to your device's speech service.

  • Offline recognition model. The app downloads a small recognition model (about 19 MB, “quran-asr-tiny” by Abdou, MIT licence) once from our website (mubi64.github.io, or huggingface.co if that fails). After that, recognition runs entirely on your phone: your voice never leaves the device, and the audio is processed in memory and not stored.
  • Your device's speech service. On iPhone this is Apple's Speech Recognition; on Android it is Google's. The app passes the audio to that service, which processes it under its own privacy policy and, depending on your device, language and settings, may send it to Apple or Google's servers. QuranMate does not control this and does not receive the audio. If you want your voice to stay on your phone, download the offline model when the app offers it.

While voice reveal is listening, the app keeps the screen awake so the session is not interrupted; this involves no data. You can withdraw microphone or speech-recognition access at any time in your device's Settings.

Notifications

The optional daily review reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It involves no server.

What we do not do

We do not collect personal data, create accounts, show ads, sell or share data, track you across apps or websites, or use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app contains no ads, purchases or user-generated content shared with others, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices and your data

Because the data never leaves your device, you are in control of it: delete a bookmark, note or highlight in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything. There is nothing held about you on our side to access, correct or delete.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example if optional crash reporting is added), this policy and the in-app text will be updated first and any such feature will require your explicit opt-in.
