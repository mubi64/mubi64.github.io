QuranMate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

QuranMate keeps everything on your device.

What the app downloads

On first launch it downloads the Quran text and translations once from a public GitHub repository (raw.githubusercontent.com/fawazahmed0/quran-api). Recitation audio is streamed from everyayah.com only when you press play, and can be saved on your device for offline listening. These servers see an ordinary web request (IP address, user-agent) as any website would; QuranMate sends them nothing about you.

What stays on your phone

Your bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading statistics (pages, time, streak), Hifz progress and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use voice reveal in Hifz mode, the app asks for microphone access. Speech recognition is performed by your device's own speech service (for example Google speech recognition, according to your device settings). If you turn on offline voice recognition, the app downloads a small recognition model (about 19 MB) once from the developer's website (mubi64.github.io, or huggingface.co if that fails) and recognition then runs entirely on your phone. Either way QuranMate does not record, store or transmit your voice. While voice reveal is listening, the app keeps the screen awake so the session is not interrupted; this involves no data.

Notifications

The optional daily review reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device.

What we do not do

We do not collect personal data, create accounts, show ads, sell data, or use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app contains no ads or user-generated content and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example if optional crash reporting is added), this policy and the in-app text will be updated first and any such feature will require your explicit opt-in.
